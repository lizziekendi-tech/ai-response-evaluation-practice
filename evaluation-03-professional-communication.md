AI Response Evaluation 03: Professional Communication

Task

A user asks an AI:

"Write a professional email asking my manager for two days off because of a family commitment."

Response A

"Hey boss, I won't be coming in for two days. Something came up with my family. Hope that's cool."

Response B

"Dear Manager,

I would like to request two days of leave due to a family commitment. Please let me know if you require any additional information or if there are procedures I should follow.

Thank you for your consideration.

Kind regards."

Evaluation

Preferred response: B

Response B is more professional and appropriately frames the message as a request rather than an announcement.

Problems identified in Response A:

- Uses an overly casual tone for a professional workplace email.
- Presents the absence as a decision rather than a request.
- Does not show appropriate professional courtesy.

Suggested improvement

Response B could be improved by including:

- The specific dates requested.
- The manager's name, if known.
- A clear subject line, such as "Request for Two Days of Leave."

Conclusion

Response B better satisfies the user's request because it uses an appropriate professional tone and clearly communicates the intention to request leave.

Skills demonstrated

- Tone assessment
- Professional communication
- Instruction-following evaluation
- Identification of missing information
- Constructive response improvement
