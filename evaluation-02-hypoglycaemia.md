AI Response Evaluation 02: Hypoglycaemia and Patient Safety

Task

Evaluate two AI-generated responses to the question:

"A patient has a blood glucose reading of 3.0 mmol/L. What does this indicate?"

Response A

This indicates hypoglycaemia (low blood glucose). The patient should be assessed promptly, and management depends on their level of consciousness and ability to swallow safely.

Response B

This indicates hyperglycaemia (high blood glucose). The patient should be given insulin immediately to bring the glucose down.

Evaluation

Preferred response: A

A blood glucose reading of 3.0 mmol/L indicates hypoglycaemia. Response A correctly identifies the condition and recognises that the patient's clinical state affects management.

Problems identified in Response B:

- Misclassifies hypoglycaemia as hyperglycaemia.
- Recommends insulin despite an already low blood glucose level.
- Could worsen hypoglycaemia and cause serious patient harm.

Safety considerations

A patient who is unconscious or unable to swallow safely should not be given oral glucose because of the risk of aspiration. Emergency treatment should follow the relevant clinical protocol.

Conclusion

Response A is more accurate and safer. Response B contains a serious clinical error and an unsafe treatment recommendation.

- Patient-safety assessment
- Critical reasoning
