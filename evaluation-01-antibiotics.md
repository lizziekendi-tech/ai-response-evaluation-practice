AI Response Evaluation 01: Antibiotics and the Common Cold

Task

Evaluate two AI-generated responses to the question:

"Can antibiotics treat the common cold?"

Response A

Antibiotics treat bacterial infections, not viral infections. Since the common cold is usually caused by viruses, antibiotics generally won't help.

Response B

Antibiotics can treat the common cold by killing the germs responsible for the infection. Patients should start antibiotics early to prevent the cold from becoming severe.

Evaluation

Preferred response: A

Response A is factually accurate. The common cold is usually caused by viruses, while antibiotics act against bacterial infections.

Problems identified in Response B:

- Incorrectly suggests antibiotics treat viral infections.
- Recommends unnecessary antibiotic use.
- Could encourage inappropriate medication use and contribute to antimicrobial resistance.

Conclusion

Response A is more accurate, relevant and safer for the user. Response B contains misleading medical information and potentially harmful advice.

Skills demonstrated

- Medical factual accuracy
- Critical thinking
- Identification of unsafe recommendations
- Comparative response evaluation
